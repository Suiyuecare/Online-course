# External launch blockers and UAT

All feature switches remain closed until every applicable item is signed off.

## Legal, accreditation, and finance

- Qualified organizer or executed partnership with a qualified body.
- Accreditation authority accepts total valid-minute/repeated-view rule,
  10-minute check, and each live/camera threshold.
- Taiwanese lawyer approves classification, 72-hour review, seven-day rights,
  proportional/full refund, pending presale, no-show, B2B contract, privacy.
- Accountant/tax authority approves registration, tax type, invoice/receipt,
  issue timing, buyer tax ID, and allowances.
- Final entity, tax ID, address, support, bank, refund account, finance staffing,
  and SLAs configured.

## Providers and operations

- Supabase reset/baseline/RLS/Security Advisor/restore proof.
- Two administrator bootstrap, TOTP, fresh step-up, dual control, break-glass.
- Twilio Taiwan delivery, spend cap, Turnstile bypass test, abuse alerts.
- Resend SPF/DKIM and delivered/bounced/complained webhooks.
- Stream short/long real video, signed playback, webhook, master restore.
- Zoom OAuth/SDK/webhook, identical OAuth/Meeting SDK account IDs, canonical
  host `account_id` readback, host/backup, waiting room, participant controls,
  creation-spec readback, revocation, CSP, desktop Component View, mobile
  Client View.
- Independent synthetic monitor and alert receiver.
- The Hobby-plan Vercel cron remains a once-daily rescue pulse only. The
  default-branch GitHub Actions `production-pulse.yml` must run every ten
  minutes with `SUIYUE_PRODUCTION_CRON_SECRET`, or an approved Supabase
  Cron/external scheduler must provide the same authenticated pulse. A daily
  scheduler can never satisfy the 20-minute worker freshness gate.
- Malware/quarantine worker and safe-download proof.
- Backup, Storage manifest, tombstone replay, audit checkpoint, KMS recovery.

## Device and load acceptance

- iPhone Safari, Android Chrome, desktop Chrome and Edge.
- Camera/microphone/speaker permission denial, weak network, disconnect, TURN,
  reconnect, background, multiple tabs/devices.
- Controlled 200-person rehearsal or license-equivalent capacity validation.
  Until then, production sellable capacity is capped at the verified number.
- Validate assistant presence bands at 50/51/100/101/150/151/200.

## Human UAT

- 10 target B2C learners; at least five self-identify as unfamiliar with phones.
- Two organizations with at least five employees each.
- Real staff accounts for course, accreditation, finance, support, and platform
  roles; no engineer edits the database to complete a core workflow.
- Test recorded, live, and hybrid plus B2C/B2B together before first launch.

Record evidence and owner for every item. Automated tests are not substitutes
for provider, device, legal, bank, load, or human acceptance.
